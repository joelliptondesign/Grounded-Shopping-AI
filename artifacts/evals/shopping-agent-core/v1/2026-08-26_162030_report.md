## Observed issues worth reviewing before the polished prototype

- Calibrate qualitative target/priority extraction so “cooling matters most” and similar language maps consistently to the versioned gold state.
- Improve contextual follow-up resolution for “it,” “that,” “the result,” and “eligible” without unnecessary clarification.
- Preserve the active catalog-versus-review subject across successive review questions; the wrong-product answer in `journey_reviews_001` is the clearest critical issue.
- Carry the active service topic to a newly named product and answer unknown availability as unknown.
- Reduce validator/fallback friction caused by generation requesting facts or review themes absent from the supplied evidence.
- Review exact-clarification expectations separately from semantically equivalent wording failures before changing either prompts or scoring.
